# VIRYA Art Bible

The visual north star is dark comic photorealism with a theatrical, psychological, human presence. Every frame should belong to VIRYA Signal: readable silhouettes, steel-blue foundations, selective pink and warm-gold accents, and organic darkness.

## Pillars

- Give every room one unmistakable focal subject and hero moment.
- Treat people or their symbols as narrative anchors, not anonymous decoration.
- Build depth in 2.5D: foreground creates impact, subject carries story, background establishes place.
- Use theatrical rim light and one directed source; reserve bloom for hero beats.
- Keep UI and world art in the same signal language: fine technical lines, waveform/ring motifs, stains, grain, and strong contrast.
- Keep character layers modular so future Viryatkowo costumes can be introduced without rebuilding a room.

## Palette and scale

Core colors are steel cyan `#71DCFF`, signal pink `#E35F83`, warm gold `#F0CF88`, near-black `#0A1018`, and slate `#1A2230`. Masters target 1080 x 1920 for scenes and subjects, 864 x 1536 for foreground layers, and 648 x 1152 for parallax backgrounds. Runtime scene exports target 675 x 1200.

## Character archetypes

- Signal Bearer / Mr R: guitar, cables, diagnostic light; initiates change and guides attention.
- Voice Under Mask / Yanusin: microphone, masks, ribbons; carries the tension between concealment and expression.
- Warden of Rhythm / Koobosky: sticks, medical monitors, mechanical impulses; gives scenes pulse and controlled chaos.
- Anchor of Weight / Lubek: bass, industrial lamps, steel frames; grounds dreamlike scenes in physical presence.

The canonical character copy, costume hooks, room ownership, and color values live in `data/virya_world.json`.
