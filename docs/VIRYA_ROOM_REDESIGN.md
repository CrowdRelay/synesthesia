## Composition rules

Gameplay targets must stay readable within the portrait safe area. Foreground elements may frame or cross the scene but must not intercept unrelated pointer input. The subject layer carries character and costume hooks; environmental reactions live in props, particles, masks, and localized shaders. Post-reveal motion begins during the hero settle and continues after completion.

## Review checklist

- The room is identifiable from its silhouette and dominant light alone.
- Its assigned VIRYA archetype or symbolic presence is apparent.
- The mechanic target reads before instructional UI is needed.
- Background, subject, foreground, and props produce real depth at portrait and ultrawide crops.
- Completion creates a stronger living composition rather than a frozen reveal.
- Optional echoes remain discoverable after the main mechanic finishes.
