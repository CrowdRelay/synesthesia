# Synesthesia — Living Rooms V4

This pass locks the visual direction from the approved Signal moodboard and moves the rooms from static illustrated cards toward living interactive spaces.

## What changed

- Six weak/repetitive rooms were rebuilt at asset level: Party Time, The Calling, Waves, Rise, Hybrid and From the Ashes.
- The Calling no longer draws literal wine goblets. Its interaction is a resonance ritual: charge the basin, move the signal node, close the sigil.
- Party Time keeps the overload metaphor but replaces glossy game-like balloons with fragile sensory membranes, ribbons and burst fragments.
- Waves no longer draws human stick figures. Two abstract waveform presences breathe, respond and enter phase while curtains and rain move around them.
- Rise no longer draws a procedural person. Ascension is communicated through architectural light, rising debris, sequential steps and expanding rings.
- Hybrid has a mechanical frequency core instead of reusing the band-avatar portal wall.
- From the Ashes restores the phoenix as the hero instead of the shared portal composition.
- Legacy cinematic video is disabled for the six V4 procedural-living rooms so old props cannot bleed back over the new art.

## Living-world rule

Every room has exactly one visual signature and a different ambient motion language. Motion stays subtle: the authored image is still the hero, but no room should feel frozen when the player stops touching the screen.
