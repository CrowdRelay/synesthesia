# Synesthesia — full room gameplay pass

The album is an explorable interactive space, not a paint-progress meter. Every room follows the same player promise:

**notice → touch/manipulate → immediate audiovisual response → changed world state → optional echo → room payoff.**

## Rooms
- **Wave of Uncertainty** — guide the wave laterally until the horizon stabilizes.
- **Party Time** — tap, push and slash through physical balloons; confetti and mix bloom respond per pop.
- **Unmasked** — crack masks, grab them and physically slide them off the wall.
- **The Calling** — hold the bottle to pour, grab the glass, move it into the toast.
- **Seed of Doubt** — find the seed at floor level, touch/hold it, then guide growth upward; crown auto-resolves once growth is sufficient.
- **Hybrid** — hold the opponent to stabilize the aim, then release the choice.
- **Technophobia** — pull cables, kill the breaker, tune the signal; screens/noise react causally.
- **Invaluable** — crack a mirror, then drag/swipe the loosened pane off the wall.
- **From the Ashes** — swirl ash into shape; enough energy releases the phoenix, upward gesture can accelerate it.
- **Waves** — discover another presence, then bring two breathing points closer with one or two fingers.
- **Rise** — tap the light, hold the atrium center, finish with an upward gesture.

## Exploration rules
- interaction targets are not permanently highlighted;
- after inactivity or repeated misses, thin Signal rings reveal only the current meaningful targets;
- physical props capture the pointer so the reveal brush never paints underneath a manipulation;
- echoes remain optional and are **not auto-awarded when the door opens**;
- after completion a player may stay and search, continue, or revisit via Album Mode.
