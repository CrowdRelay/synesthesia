## App name
Synesthesia by VIRYA

## Short description
An interactive journey through Echoes Of The Modern Mind — touch, sound and image.

## Full description
Synesthesia is an interactive VIRYA experience built around the album Echoes Of The Modern Mind.

Each chapter combines music, image, touch and haptics in a short mechanical scene set inside a raw psychiatric-ward world. It is not a traditional game or a music player — it is a way to move through the album with your own hands.

Inside you will find:

• eleven interactive chapters inspired by VIRYA songs,
• a hand-authored world of psychiatric wards and therapy spaces,
• gestures, motion, sound and haptics that react to how you interact,
• local progress persistence and replayable completed chapters,
• a finale connected with Virya Signal,
• adaptive performance modes that scale effects to the device.

Synesthesia is designed as a mobile art experience. Headphones are recommended, but the experience does not depend on advertising or continuous user tracking.
